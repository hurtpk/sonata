What activation shares with Sonata's owner
  • Your Discord user ID and profile name.
  • Your Sonata version and first activation time.
  • Your last check-in, updated about every five minutes while Sonata runs.

The owner can deny access to individual accounts. No Discord messages, friends or servers are sent. Allowed records expire after 90 days without a check-in; blocked IDs are retained until the owner removes the restriction.
